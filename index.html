📖 How to Play

01
Drive your boat

Steer with WASD / arrow keys, drag the left joystick on mobile, boost with Shift or ⚡. Sail to each island and fish its waters!

02
Cast & reel

Tap 🎣 CAST, then HOLD REEL when the fish icon drifts into the white zone at the center of the vertical bar. The fish icon's color shows its rarity — better rods catch rarer fish!

03
Weather & time

Some fish only bite at certain times of day or in certain weather. Moonrain doubles your luck; Ancient Tide boosts relics.

04
Sell fish

Open your inventory to sell fish for coins. Upgrade rods, lines & bobbers at each island's shop.

05
Complete quests

Islanders want specific fish. Complete quests for coins, XP, titles and even boat unlocks!

06
The Altar

Off Crescent Isle, spend relics to enchant your rod. 200 pity points guarantee a Legendary.

07
The Baby Leviathan Hunt

Every hour (UTC), a baby Leviathan appears in the open waters southwest of Tanglewood for 10 minutes. Every catch in its zone damages its shared health. The target starts at 30 health solo and gains 14 health for each extra angler in the zone, so every crew can finish it. Land the final catch to claim the Leviathan Eye!

08
Codes & titles

Redeem codes in the shop menu and unlock titles by catching, selling, leveling & filling the index.

09
Multiplayer & chat

You share the ocean with other anglers in real time — sail together and see their nameplates. Tap 💬 in the top bar to open Harbor Chat; messages pop up in bubbles over everyone's head.
